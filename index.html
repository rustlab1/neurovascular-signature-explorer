Neurovascular Molecular Signatures in Neurodegeneration

Shared and disease-specific human brain vascular signatures in Alzheimer's disease, frontotemporal dementia (FTD-GRN) and Huntington's disease

Interactive companion to a comparative single-nucleus RNA-seq study of human brain vascular and perivascular cells in three public datasets: Alzheimer's disease (AD), GRN-related frontotemporal dementia (FTD-GRN) and Huntington's disease (HD). Each dataset was analysed as a non-neuronal atlas of eight cell types, with endothelial cells resolved into arterial, capillary and venous zones and pericytes into transport-type (T-peri) and matrix-type (M-peri) subtypes. Differential expression versus matched controls was computed with MAST; a positive log2 fold-change means higher expression in disease. All numbers on this page are read from the final result tables and objects of the analysis.
